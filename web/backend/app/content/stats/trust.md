# Trust

**Range:** −5–5 | **Default:** 0

Trust captures a character's standing with their close allies and
companions — not public reputation (which belongs to Suspicion) but the
personal credibility they have earned or squandered through their actions.

## Interpreting the scale

The scale runs from deep mistrust (−5) through neutral (0) to strong
personal trust (+5). Zero means the relationship is transactional and
unproven; neither goodwill nor ill-will has accumulated.

| Range   | Tone             | What it implies                                              |
|---------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| −5–−3   | Betrayed         | The ally considers the character a liability or enemy.       |
|         |                  | Cooperation is strained or impossible; they may warn others. |
| −2–−1   | Skeptical        | Past missteps have left a mark. The ally is guarded and      |
|         |                  | expects the character to disappoint again.                   |
| 0       | Neutral          | No track record — or a mixed one that has balanced out.      |
| 1–2     | Earned trust     | The character has demonstrated reliability. The ally gives   |
|         |                  | them some benefit of the doubt.                              |
| 3–5     | Deep trust       | The ally would vouch for, cover for, or take risks for       |
|         |                  | the character without hesitation.                            |

## Agent guidance

Trust shifts slowly. A single act of courage or loyalty might earn +1;
betrayal typically costs 2–3 points and is hard to repair. Avoid moving
Trust more than ±2 from any single scene event unless the act is
unambiguous and witnessed. At −4 or lower, the ally relationship is
effectively broken and a reconciliation arc is required to recover it.
